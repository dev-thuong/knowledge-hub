# #1. Introduction to the AshenRealm Project

**AshenRealm** is a personal Minecraft server that I am developing for entertainment purposes. This document logs the development process of AshenRealm from scratch.

**AshenRealm** is not an official product and is not affiliated with **Mojang Studios** or **Microsoft** in any way.

> Note: This article was translated using AI; the original version was written in Vietnamese.

---

## My Goals

In terms of administration:

* Players interact with and keep each other in check, developing on their own according to established rules without needing much help from the staff
* No Admins, aiming to fully automate administrative tasks

<br>

In terms of content:

* A free-to-play server where everything sold can be grinded for and isn't too difficult to get
* A well-crafted storyline, but players are not forced to follow it
* Everyone is free to do what they like
* The world does not completely reset; instead, players are allowed to accumulate within permitted limits.
* Inherits the content and philosophy of vanilla Minecraft

<br>

## Added Features

* Age and life stages directly affect player appearance/stats
* Ability to respawn / reincarnate / completely reset (delete account)
* Attribute system, with each attribute granting players a different ability
* Weapon and pet systems
* Cultivation and title systems tied to the world
* Different worlds, each featuring a completely unique style

<br>

## Main Lore

> In the year 3026, abnormal creatures have drained the energy from the core of the Sun, causing it to swell into a Red Giant over the next few centuries.
>  
> With science hitting a bottleneck, humanity resolutely refuses to migrate to other planets. Instead, they dig in on Earth beneath protective domes, accepting a life-or-death battle against time. They establish the **Global Human Alliance**, led by a superintelligent AI to run the grand strategy spanning thousands of years.  
>  
> Humans send warriors/geniuses (the players) to a floating island in space (the transit station) to teleport into the Spirit Realm – an ally in another dimension – to learn secret arts and bring them back to defeat the anomalies and save Earth.  
>
> In the Spirit Realm, players must join the fight to push back a Zombie pandemic in exchange for knowledge and trust from its inhabitants.  

> Underlying plot: The supreme AI has actually turned its back on humanity. It believes that the old human race is too weak and stubborn. The AI is exploiting the Zombie gene from the Spirit Realm, combining it with its own genetic technology to engineer a "Perfect New Human Species" capable of living directly under solar radiation, quietly preparing for a grand purge of the old humanity.

Names and Plans:

- **Lyra** (The Spirit Realm) named after the Lyra constellation. The cities are arranged according to the constellation's pattern. Exclusive items: Lute/Lyre (weapon), Wings at the central city, Sword at the outer cities. Exclusive pets: Parrot, Zombie. Quests will be based on the Spirit Realm's anti-zombie storyline.

> A lute/lyre is a fitting weapon for the Lyra constellation, and I've seen it featured in some pretty cool mods before, but it doesn't seem very practical

- **Aether Nexus** (The Floating Island) this is the world_hub, an island in space where players spawn. The exclusive item is an armor set, meant to remind players not to "die" too early. It also sells some skins suitable for different realms. Quests will be based on the main lore written above.
